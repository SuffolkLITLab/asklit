Voice for participants

Participants are never required to use voice and cannot change these choices. Browser voice is free and needs no setup. Paid service needs your organization’s administrator to enable paid voice, and counts against the monthly AI budget; it applies only if you choose it here. OpenAI and Amazon Polly neural cost about the same per spoken minute; OpenAI is usually more natural and expressive. Providers your host has not configured are unavailable. Browser voices follow the participant’s device, so the feminine/masculine choice is matched by voice name and is best effort there; the paid AI voices follow it exactly. Changes reach participants when you publish.
