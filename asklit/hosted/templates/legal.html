Privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026

This notice describes the hosted AskLit service operated by the Legal Innovation & Technology Lab at Suffolk University Law School. AskLit lets people create, manage, publish, and use AI assistants. Other websites and independently hosted copies may have different policies.

Information we receive

When you sign in with Google, we receive your account identifier, name, email address, verification status, and, when supplied, Google Workspace domain. We use this information to sign you in and apply organization access rules. AskLit requests only basic identity scopes (openid, email, and profile); it does not request access to your Gmail, Drive files, or contacts. Microsoft sign-in, when configured, similarly uses identity and tenant information.

For local accounts, we store your name, email used as a username, organization membership, and a salted password hash. We also store AskLit configurations, knowledge-base files and extracted text, published revisions, evaluation inputs and results, and administrative activity records. We record model-call counts, status, token counts, model identifiers, and estimated costs by organization and assistant to monitor usage and recover hosting and AI costs. Usage records do not include message or answer text.

During a conversation, we process and store messages, responses, and extracted text from attachments. Service providers also process connection information such as IP addresses. AskLit uses security and usage counters to limit abuse and costs.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate accounts, enforce access controls, retrieve relevant knowledge-base content, generate answers, evaluate assistants, and maintain the service. AI requests send conversation content, relevant knowledge-base excerpts, instructions, and attachment text to the configured model provider. This deployment uses Amazon Bedrock in Suffolk’s AWS account, with operator-selected models from Amazon and other model providers. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share with the service and its providers.

Fly.io hosts the application. Supabase provides database and private file storage. Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS. Amazon Web Services processes model requests through Bedrock. Google processes Google sign-in. These providers process information needed to supply their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Organization administrators can manage their organization's AskLits and memberships. Published assistants may disclose information drawn from their knowledge bases in answers. Public listings and public assistant links are accessible to anyone; an unlisted link is not an access restriction. Password and organization restrictions limit access but do not make an AI conversation a confidential legal consultation. Service operators can access stored information as needed for operation, support, and security.

Voice

Browser speech features may send audio or text to your browser or operating-system provider; processing is not necessarily confined to your device. If an organization enables paid speech, recordings are sent to the configured transcription provider and reply text to the speech provider when configured. Paid speech is currently unavailable on this deployment; browser speech remains available. AskLit does not persist these audio recordings as account files. Transcribed text becomes part of the conversation when sent.

Cookies and browser storage

AskLit uses cookies for sign-in sessions and OAuth security, and browser storage for conversation access and interface preferences. These are used to operate the service. The Studio does not include advertising or analytics trackers. An embedding website may use its own cookies and analytics.

Retention and deletion

Hosted conversations expire {{ retention_days }} days after creation and are removed by background housekeeping. Clearing a conversation through the chat controls deletes its stored conversation record and related messages and attachments. Account information, assistant configurations, knowledge-base documents, revisions, evaluations, and administrative records do not all share that automatic expiration. Archiving an assistant is not the same as deleting its stored data.

Contact litlab@suffolk.edu to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with your organization. Backup copies and provider records may persist under their separate retention processes; information may also need to be retained for security or legal reasons. Removing AskLit's access in your Google account prevents future Google authorization but does not delete existing AskLit records.

Contact and changes

Questions about this notice can be sent to litlab@suffolk.edu. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. See also the Suffolk LIT Lab website policy.

Hosting approval and fees

Authors may not host an AskLit for free on the suffolklitlab.org domain. Hosted author accounts require LIT Lab approval and a small fee to recover hosting and AI usage costs. Email litlab@suffolk.edu to request approval. We will discuss the fee, included usage, and any additional usage charges with you before enabling hosting. Creating a sign-in identity does not grant hosting approval or start a payment.

We monitor request volume, token usage, and estimated AI costs. Usage allowances and hosting approval may be adjusted or suspended. Exact charges and payment arrangements are agreed separately; the usage dashboard is an estimate of provider cost, not an invoice. These author-hosting fees do not by themselves charge visitors for using a published assistant.

Using AskLit

AskLit provides tools to create and use AI assistants. You are responsible for your account credentials, the content you submit, and the assistants you publish. Use only materials you have permission to upload and share. Respect other people's privacy and intellectual property. Do not use the service for unlawful activity, to bypass access controls, to distribute malicious files, or to disrupt the service.

AI answers and legal information

AI answers can be incorrect, incomplete, or out of date, including when they cite sources. Check important information against reliable sources before relying on it. Do not enter sensitive identifying information, such as bank account numbers, government ID numbers, or passwords. AskLit does not provide legal representation, create an attorney-client relationship, or guarantee confidentiality or legal privilege. Using an assistant does not file a document with a court or meet a deadline. Consult a qualified professional when needed.

Your content and publishing

You are responsible for choosing appropriate access settings and reviewing an assistant's instructions, knowledge bases, and behavior before publishing. Public and unlisted assistants can be accessed by people outside your organization; shared passwords can be passed on by recipients.

Organizations and availability

Organization administrators manage membership, assistants, and optional paid features within their organization. Usage limits apply. We may restrict or suspend access to address abuse, security issues, excessive usage, or violations of these terms. Features may change or become unavailable. Keep copies of material you need; do not rely on this service as your only storage.

Contact and changes

For support, concerns about content, or questions about these terms, contact litlab@suffolk.edu. Changes to these terms will be posted here with an updated date.
