Massachusetts Public Benefits: Eligibility and Application Guide

SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program)
SNAP provides monthly food assistance benefits loaded onto an EBT card. To be eligible in Massachusetts, a household must meet gross income limits (generally 200% of the Federal Poverty Level) and net income limits (100% FPL). Many households with an elderly or disabled member are exempt from the net income test. Apply online at DTAConnect.com, by phone at 877-382-2363, or in person at a local DTA office.

MASSHEALTH (Medicaid)
MassHealth provides free or low-cost health insurance to Massachusetts residents. Eligibility depends on income, household size, age, and immigration status. Families with incomes up to 133% FPL and children up to 300% FPL may qualify for free coverage. Apply online at MAhealthconnector.org or call 800-841-2900.

TAFDC (Transitional Aid to Families with Dependent Children)
TAFDC provides cash assistance to low-income families with children. To be eligible, families must have children under 18 (or under 19 if in school), meet income and asset limits, and comply with work requirements (with some exceptions for caretakers of young children or those with disabilities). Benefits are time-limited to 24 months in a 60-month period.

EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE (EA)
Emergency Assistance provides short-term shelter to homeless families with children. Families must have a child under 21, be legally present in the U.S., meet income requirements (below 115% FPL), and be homeless or at immediate risk of homelessness. Apply by calling the DTA Emergency Assistance line at 866-584-0653.

FUEL ASSISTANCE (LIHEAP)
The Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program helps households pay heating bills. Income eligibility is generally up to 60% of state median income. Benefits are credited directly to your utility or fuel account. Apply through your local Community Action Agency starting in November.
